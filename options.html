Right Click Ipsum

By default, generated text always starts with "Lorem ipsum..."

If enabled, a random sentence from the source text is used as the starting point instead of always starting with "Lorem ipsum..."

Email Domain

Override the domain used for generated email addresses. Leave blank to use the default (example.com).

Keyboard Shortcuts

Ctrl + Shift + L

Opens a popup in the focused text field with smart suggestions and lorem ipsum options.

Ctrl + Shift + K

Instantly auto-fills the current form with fake data. Click on any form field first, then press the shortcut.

Customize shortcuts at chrome://extensions/shortcuts

Screenshot Upload

Configure your bearer token for uploading screenshots to the image service. Press Ctrl + Shift + S to capture a region.
